# Procurement policy — laboratory instruments and consumables

Halden Instruments Ltd · Internal · Policy FIN-07 · Version 6 · Effective 2026-01-01

This policy governs what the company buys for its own laboratories and service
operation. It does not govern what customers buy from us.

## Thresholds

| Order value (excluding VAT) | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Up to £2,500 | Budget holder's approval only |
| £2,501 – £25,000 | Two written quotes, budget holder plus finance |
| £25,001 – £100,000 | Three written quotes, finance director |
| Over £100,000 | Three written quotes, finance director and one board member |

A single requirement may not be split into several orders to stay under a
threshold. Two orders to the same supplier within 30 days for related items are
treated as one order for the purposes of this table; finance reviews this
monthly and has reversed four orders on these grounds since the policy came into
force.

## Approved suppliers

Consumables must be bought from an approved supplier unless no approved supplier
can meet the requirement, in which case the exception is recorded with a reason
and reviewed at the quarterly supplier meeting.

Current approved suppliers for optical consumables: Kelbrook Scientific,
Verrall & Sons, and Northlake Supply (a separate legal entity from Northlake
Analytical Services, which carries out our independent testing — the two must
not be confused on a purchase order, and have been twice).

## Lead times to plan against

- Lamp assemblies (HAL-LMP-4419): 6 weeks, and 10 weeks between November and
  January, when the supplier's own production pauses.
- Standard cartridges: 2 weeks.
- High-sensitivity cartridges: 5 weeks, single-sourced from Verrall & Sons.
- Carousels: 3 weeks.

Consumables are ordered quarterly, against a forecast from the service desk. The
forecast has run 15–20 % under actual usage in each of the last three quarters,
which is why the lamp buffer stock was exhausted in February 2026 and two
customer visits were delayed.

## Single-sourcing

High-sensitivity cartridges have one supplier. This has been on the risk
register as a medium risk since 2024-11, with a mitigation — qualify a second
supplier — that has been carried forward through five quarterly reviews without
being started. The February 2026 shortage was of lamps, not cartridges, so the
single-source risk has still not been tested.

## Emergency purchases

Where a customer instrument is down and the part is not in stock, the service
manager may authorise an emergency purchase up to £10,000 outside the quote
requirements, provided finance is notified the same day and the reason is
recorded. This route was used seven times in the twelve months to March 2026,
five of those for lamp assemblies during the February shortage.
